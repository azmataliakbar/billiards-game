How to play (easy rules)
  1. The table has 15 balls: solids 1–7, stripes 9–15, and the black 8 ball.
  2. Break: the first player hits the rack. The first group ball pocketed decides the groups.
  3. Your first contact must be a ball of your group. The turn line tells you which group to hit now.
  4. Pocket all your balls, then pocket the 8 ball to win.
  5. Foul (no ball hit, wrong ball first, or the cue ball pocketed): the turn passes and the cue ball goes behind the line.
  6. Pocketing the 8 ball early, or on a foul, loses the game.

Aim: press on the cue ball, pull back, and let go. The dashed line shows the path. Green means the first ball is yours, red means it isn't.
